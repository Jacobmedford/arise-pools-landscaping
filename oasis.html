Who we are

Designed outdoors. Built once. Enjoyed forever.

For over 15 years, Arise has been building custom pools, landscapes and outdoor living spaces across the Valley. What started as a single pool crew is now a full design and build studio, and the rule hasn't changed: design it right, build it once.

We're not a volume builder. We take on a limited number of projects each year, so you get one dedicated team from the first call to the day you jump in.
