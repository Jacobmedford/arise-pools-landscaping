Pools & Landscapes in San Tan Valley

Building a Pool in San Tan Valley

San Tan Valley sits east of the San Tan Mountains in Pinal County, and it is where we built Legacy Living, a full-lot resort with a pool house and a lit pickleball court. First pool or builder-grade update, the design starts with the lot you actually have.

The permit picture is changing. Voters approved incorporating San Tan Valley in August 2025, and Pinal County has announced that starting November 1, 2026, all permitting for properties inside the new Town of San Tan Valley goes through the Town. Homes in the unincorporated area around it stay with Pinal County Building Safety. We confirm which office covers your address before anything is filed.

Associations are the other piece. Johnson Ranch, for one, publishes its own forms and guidelines. We run any HOA review alongside the permit, and our San Tan Valley builder guide walks through a full project.

Design Notes

What San Tan Valley Yards Ask For

Lessons from Legacy Living that travel well to neighborhood lots.

01

One Ground Plane

Large travertine pavers with narrow turf ribbons between them give a deck life without a full lawn to water and mow. The pattern works on a patio of any size, and the pale stone stays comfortable underfoot in summer.

02

A Spa That Spills Over

A raised spa that sheets into the pool takes less room than a separate one, shares plumbing and equipment, and adds the sound of moving water. It is one of the most useful ideas to carry from a big lot to a smaller one.

03

Courts on Their Own Level

At Legacy Living the pickleball court sits below the pool terrace, reached by lit travertine steps, so play never takes over the view from the house. On a standard lot, a putting green or a turf game area can borrow the same idea.

Good Questions

Questions From San Tan Valley Homeowners

Who issues pool permits in San Tan Valley now?

It depends on your address. From November 1, 2026, permitting inside the Town of San Tan Valley moves from Pinal County to the Town; homes outside town limits stay with Pinal County Building Safety. We confirm the right office first.

Do I need HOA approval to build a pool?

If your home is in a community association, plan on design review. We read your association's rules early and submit alongside the permit.

Is Legacy Living in Johnson Ranch?

No. Legacy Living is one of our San Tan Valley projects, on a larger property than most neighborhood lots. We use it to show ideas that scale down, such as turf ribbons, a spillover spa and lighting built into the masonry.

Can a smaller San Tan Valley yard still feel like a resort?

Yes. A raised spa attached to the pool, a short plant list repeated well, a pergola for shade and lighting set into steps and walls give a neighborhood lot much of that feeling.

Can my pool be controlled from my phone?

Yes. We build with Pentair equipment, so lighting scenes, spa heat and pump schedules can all run from an app. It is part of our smart automation and cleaning systems work.
