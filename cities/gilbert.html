Pools & Landscapes in Gilbert

Building a Pool in Gilbert

Gilbert is home. Our office sits in the 85295 ZIP, and most yards we design here are walled and rectangular, sized for a family. The pool shares that lot with a patio, a grill, grass for the kids and the path to the side gate, so good design here means fitting it all in without crowding.

Every new pool needs a Town of Gilbert permit, and Development Services handles pool and spa permits through its One Stop Shop. The Town's code sets the enclosure too: walls or fencing five to six feet tall with self-closing, self-latching gates. If you are in an HOA, we prepare its review package alongside the permit.

Each Gilbert master plan has its own personality. Val Vista Lakes is built around water, Agritopia around a working organic farm, Morrison Ranch on land the Morrison family farmed for generations. We take cues from that setting so the stone, plants and lighting feel at home on your street.

Design Notes

What Gilbert Yards Ask For

Three things we plan around on almost every Gilbert lot.

01

Evenings Come First

Gilbert summer afternoons are for staying inside, so a yard earns its keep after sunset. We set lights into step risers, under the coping and along raised walls as the masonry goes up, wired to Pentair controls you run from your phone.

02

Room for Everyone

A family yard has to hold a swim, a lawn for the kids and shade for the adults. We give each activity its own ground: travertine by the water, turf for games, a pergola or covered bar a few steps from the pool.

03

Remodel or Start Over

Many Gilbert homes came with a builder pool. If the shell and plumbing are sound, a remodel can change the whole yard with new paving, a reworked entry and fresh water features. When the pool sits in the wrong place, we say so.

Good Questions

Questions From Gilbert Homeowners

Who issues pool permits in Gilbert?

The Town of Gilbert. Development Services reviews pool and spa permits, and the Town's code sets the enclosure rules. We prepare the plans, handle the submittal and confirm current requirements for your address.

Does my Gilbert HOA need to approve a pool?

In an association, almost always. Power Ranch, Val Vista Lakes and Seville each have review steps, so we start the HOA package during design.

Can you remodel the pool that came with my house?

Often, yes. The Hidden Jewel is a Gilbert remodel that changed the whole yard. It depends on the shell, the plumbing and how far the layout moves, which we check on site.

How long does a Gilbert pool project take?

Permits and engineering usually take two to four weeks, then construction runs eight to fourteen weeks with weekly updates. You get a schedule for your own yard once the design is set.

What will a custom pool cost in Gilbert?

Scope sets the number. Refreshing an existing pool sits at the lower end, and a new pool with a spa, lighting and an outdoor kitchen sits much higher. Your 3D proposal gives a real figure, and financing is available.
