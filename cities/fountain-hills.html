Pools & Landscapes in Fountain Hills

Building a Pool in Fountain Hills

Fountain Hills sits about 500 feet above Phoenix, rising from 1,520 feet at the fountain to 3,000 feet on Golden Eagle Boulevard, with the McDowell Mountains along its western edge. A lot of the town is built on slopes, so pools here are often terraced into the hillside, with retaining walls, stairs and views that reach as far as Four Peaks.

The Town of Fountain Hills accepts building applications online and publishes a pool checklist, a standard pool barrier detail and a separate terraced pool barrier detail for stepped yards. The barrier has to stand at least 60 inches above grade on the side facing away from the pool. On a sloped lot that measurement drives wall heights and gate locations, so we design the barrier with the terraces.

Fountain Hills became an International Dark Sky Community in 2018. Its outdoor lighting ordinance limits the color temperature of any outdoor fixture to 3000K and sets shielding rules by brightness, and pool and landscape lighting has to comply. Warm, low, shielded light is how we like an evening yard to look anyway.

Design Notes

What Fountain Hills Yards Ask For

Slope, sky and stone guide our designs here.

01

Build Into the Slope

Rather than flattening a hillside lot, we step the yard down it: a pool terrace, a spa or fire terrace above or below, and planted walls between them. It keeps grading to what the lot actually needs, and the view improves at every level.

02

Light for a Dark Sky

We specify warm fixtures at or below 3000K, shield landscape lights so they wash down rather than up, and lean on in-pool and step lights for glow without glare. Pentair scenes on a timer turn it all off late at night.

03

Stone That Matches the Hills

Desert slopes, washes and rock outcrops give Fountain Hills its look. Stacked stone, natural boulders and a desert plant palette help a new pool settle into that landscape as if it had always been there.

Good Questions

Questions From Fountain Hills Homeowners

Does Fountain Hills limit pool and landscape lighting?

Yes. As an International Dark Sky Community, the Town limits outdoor fixtures to a color temperature of 3000K and requires shielding based on light output. We design pool, step and landscape lighting to meet the ordinance.

How do pool barriers work on a sloped lot?

The barrier must stand at least 60 inches above grade on the side facing away from the pool, and the Town publishes a terraced pool barrier detail for stepped yards. We design walls, fences and gates with the terraces so they pass inspection and look intentional.

Is a pool on a Fountain Hills hillside more complex?

It takes more engineering, retaining walls and drainage planning than a flat lot, and equipment access can be tighter. In return the pool faces the view. Our hillside infinity edge guide explains the tradeoffs.

How do I apply for a pool permit in Fountain Hills?

The Town only accepts applications online. Pool submittals include a site plan, barrier details, engineering and electrical information, which we prepare and submit for you along with any HOA package your community requires.

Do you serve Eagle Mountain and FireRock?

Yes. Both are part of our Fountain Hills service area, along with the hillside neighborhoods up toward Eagle Ridge Drive. Gated golf communities usually add their own design review, and we plan it into the schedule.
