Who We Are

Fifteen Years of Outdoor Craft

Every backyard we build starts with how you want to live outside.

For over 15 years, Arise Pools & Landscaping has been creating bespoke outdoor living environments for Arizona's most discerning homeowners. What began as a single pool crew has grown into a full design-and-build studio, but our philosophy has never changed: design it right, build it once, and stand behind it forever.

We are not a volume builder. We take on a limited number of projects each year so that every client receives the attention, craftsmanship, and communication a project of this magnitude deserves. From the first discovery call to the final reveal, you work with a dedicated team that treats your home as if it were our own.
