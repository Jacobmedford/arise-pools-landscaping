Our Services

Crafted for Arizona Living

From custom pool construction to complete backyard transformations, every service is delivered with the Arise standard of excellence.

Custom Pools

Geometric, freeform, infinity edge, and plunge pools — each designed to complement your architecture and maximize your lot.

Landscape Design

Desert modern, tropical oasis, or manicured elegance — we design landscapes that thrive in Arizona's climate while looking effortlessly lush.

Outdoor Living

Kitchens, pergolas, ramadas, fire features, and entertainment spaces — extending your home into the Arizona outdoors.

Pool Remodels

Transform a tired pool into a luxury centerpiece — resurfacing, tile upgrades, automation, and complete redesigns.

Water & Fire Features

Sheer descents, waterfalls, fire bowls, and fountains — the details that transform a pool into an experience.

Hardscape & Sport Courts

Travertine decking, paver patios, retaining walls, putting greens, pickleball courts — built to last under the Arizona sun.
