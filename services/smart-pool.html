Smart Pool

Your Pool, on Your Phone

A modern pool runs on automation. Pumps, heat, lighting, water features and cleaning follow schedules you set once, and you can override any of it from an app.

We build our smart-pool systems exclusively on Pentair products and solutions. A central control system ties the equipment pad together, so warming the spa from the office, starting a color light scene for a party or pausing a water feature during a monsoon gust takes a few taps. Voice control adds hands-free commands from inside the house.

Automation also makes a pool cheaper and easier to run. Variable-speed pumps move water at lower speeds for longer, which uses far less energy than an old single-speed pump. Schedules can avoid peak utility hours, and energy-use monitoring shows what each piece of equipment costs to operate.

Cleaning systems close the loop. Depending on the pool, that might be an in-floor system that pushes debris toward the main drain, a robotic cleaner on a schedule, or salt chlorine generation that keeps sanitizer steady between visits. We recommend the right mix during design or a remodel.

Built for Arizona

Automation for Arizona Conditions

Smart equipment earns its keep in the Valley's heat, storms and cold snaps.

Summer

Run times that adapt

Long, hot days demand more filtration. Schedules lengthen run times in summer and shorten them in winter without anyone touching the equipment pad.

Utility rates

Work around peak hours

Valley utilities offer time-of-use plans with expensive peak windows. Scheduling pumps and heaters outside those hours keeps operating costs down.

Monsoon

Control from anywhere

When a storm rolls in, you can shut off water features and lighting from your phone, then run a cleaning cycle and extra filtration afterward.

Winter

Freeze protection

On the rare freezing night, automation senses the cold and keeps water moving through pipes and equipment to help prevent damage.

Smart Pool FAQ

Questions About Automation

Can automation be added to my existing pool?

Usually, yes. Most pools can be upgraded with a Pentair control system, and older pumps, heaters and lights can be replaced or tied in. We inspect your equipment pad first to see what can stay and what should be upgraded.

Do variable-speed pumps really save money?

Yes. A variable-speed pump can run at low speed for most of the day and still turn over the pool, using far less energy than an older single-speed pump. Scheduling around peak utility hours adds to the savings.

Can I control my pool with voice commands?

Yes. Pentair automation supports app and voice control, so you can turn on lights, adjust the spa or start a feature hands-free.

What is the difference between in-floor cleaning and a robotic cleaner?

An in-floor system uses pop-up heads built into the pool floor to push debris toward the main drain, so it has to be planned during construction or a major remodel. A robotic cleaner is a separate unit that scrubs and vacuums on a schedule and works in most pools.

Is a saltwater pool a good idea in Arizona?

Many owners like salt chlorine generation because it produces sanitizer steadily from salt in the water. It still needs balanced chemistry and attention to hard water, and natural stone around the pool should be sealed against salt. We help you decide during design.
