Overview

Smart Pool & Automation by Arise

Today’s luxury pool is intelligent. We design and integrate automation systems that let you control every aspect of your outdoor environment — pumps, heaters, lighting, water features, and more — from a single app.

Whether you want to warm the spa from the office or set a color-changing light scene for an evening party, our smart-pool integrations with Pentair and Jandy put effortless control in your hand.
