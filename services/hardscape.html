Hardscape & Sport Courts

Overview

Hardscape & Sport Courts by Arise

Hardscape is the skeleton of a great outdoor space. Our masons and craftsmen lay travertine, pavers, and natural stone with precision — building decking, patios, walls, and courts that perform flawlessly for decades.

From elegant pool decking to full sport courts and putting greens, every surface is engineered for drainage, heat, and longevity in the desert climate.
