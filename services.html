What We Build

Eight Disciplines, One Standard

Every service is designed and built by one team.

Every service is delivered with the Arise standard of design, engineering, and craftsmanship, from custom pool construction to complete backyard transformations.

Custom Pools

Geometric, freeform, infinity edge, and plunge pools, each designed to complement your architecture and maximize your lot.

Landscape Design

Desert modern, tropical oasis, or manicured elegance: landscapes that thrive in Arizona’s climate while looking effortlessly lush.

Pool Remodels

Transform a tired pool into a luxury centerpiece: resurfacing, tile upgrades, automation, and complete redesigns.

Outdoor Living

Kitchens, pergolas, ramadas, fire features, and entertainment spaces, extending your home into the Arizona outdoors.

Hardscape & Sport Courts

Travertine decking, paver patios, retaining walls, putting greens, and pickleball courts, built to last under the Arizona sun.

Water & Fire Features

Sheer descents, waterfalls, fire bowls, and fountains: the details that transform a pool into an experience.

Pool & Landscape Maintenance

Weekly and monthly service, equipment repair, and seasonal care that keeps your investment pristine year-round.

Smart Automation & Cleaning Systems

Total pool control at your fingertips. Manage lighting, temperature, and water features right from your phone. Built with industry leading Pentair products and solutions.
