About Us

The Arise Standard

A Letter from the Founder

When I founded Arise Pools & Landscaping over fifteen years ago, I had a simple belief: that every Arizona homeowner deserves an outdoor space built with the same care, creativity, and craftsmanship they'd expect from a custom home builder.

That belief hasn't changed. What has changed is the scope of what we can deliver. Today, Arise designs and builds complete outdoor environments — pools, landscapes, hardscapes, outdoor kitchens, sport courts, and every detail in between — for homeowners across Arizona's East Valley.

Every project begins with listening. We want to understand not just what you want your backyard to look like, but how you want it to feel. That's the Arise difference — and it's why our clients trust us with their most important investment.

— The Arise Team

Why Scottsdale

Why Scottsdale Homeowners Choose Arise

Scottsdale presents unique opportunities and challenges for outdoor design. From the expansive desert lots of North Scottsdale to the established neighborhoods near Old Town, every project requires an intimate understanding of local conditions — caliche soil layers, HOA architectural review, and the interplay of desert landscape with luxury amenities.

Arise has completed over 40 projects across Scottsdale, giving us deep familiarity with the unique requirements of communities like DC Ranch, Grayhawk, Troon, Gainey Ranch, and McCormick Ranch. We know which HOAs require pre-approval, typical soil conditions by neighborhood, and how to design pools that complement Scottsdale's desert modern and Spanish Colonial architecture.
