The Project

Inside Legacy Living

A full-lot resort: pool and raised spa, palms, turf ribbons through the deck, a lounge terrace and a lit pickleball court.

Legacy Living uses every part of its San Tan Valley lot. The main house and a separate pool house face each other across a central courtyard, and the pool sits between them, so both buildings open straight onto the water. From there the yard steps out into zones: a lounge terrace, fire pit seating, a putting green and a lit pickleball court.

The pool has clean straight edges on one side and a soft curve on the other, with a sun shelf facing the pool house. A raised spa at one corner, wrapped in stacked stone, sheets over stepped blue glass tile into the pool. Stacked-stone pillars along the edge carry water features aimed at the pool house, and a deep blue tile line traces the waterline.

Travertine pavers cover the courtyard. In places they open into a grid with turf ribbons between the stones, which softens a large deck and draws a path from the covered patio to the water. Near the palms, the same pavers float in gravel instead. Palms frame the pool and are uplit after dark, and gravel beds hold agaves, lantana and bird of paradise.

The play spaces sit around the edges. A putting green with flags runs between the lounge deck and the pool house. Fire pit seating sits on the terrace among the turf and pavers. Down a run of lit travertine steps between block walls, set below the pool terrace, is a pickleball court under light poles, with fencing and a basketball hoop. Along the back wall, a slatted metal privacy screen rises behind a row of loungers.

The front gets the same attention: a curved paver drive loops around a flagpole circle through desert plantings to a paver entry walk.
